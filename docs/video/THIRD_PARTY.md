Our own code (the scene components, the film resolver, the sound synthesiser, the scripts) is written for this repository. Animation uses Remotion's own `spring` and `interpolate`; no GSAP. The music bed and sound effects are synthesised from maths by `video-core/assets/film/sound.mjs`, so they carry no third-party licence.

## Fonts

No font files are stored in the repository. Inter, JetBrains Mono and Anton (all SIL OFL 1.1) come from the `@fontsource` npm packages in the engine install, and users add their own OFL fonts to `brand/fonts/`.
