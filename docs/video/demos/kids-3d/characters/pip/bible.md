# Pip the rabbit

- Name: Pip
- Species: rabbit
- Shape language: triangle
- Palette: #e8833a, #fff1d6, #ffe1b8, #3b2a2a
- Outfits: a tiny blue cap with a yellow button
- Catchphrase: "Hop in, there is room for two!"
- Voice pitch: 6
- Personality: Quick, cheerful and kind; Pip notices when a friend is on their own.

## Never

- Never leaves a friend out of a game.
- Never grabs; Pip always asks first.
