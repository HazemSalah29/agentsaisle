# Scene kinds

Text is always text, never HTML. In any text prop, `*word*` draws that word in the accent colour; star one or two words, not more. Items that appear one by one come in on the spoken word that names them (or on `on`, the words you choose), a moment early; with no match they follow the narration lines, then an even spread. The first item is on screen within half a second.

## Explainer kinds

| Kind           | Props                                                                                                                                                            | Use it for                                                                                                                                   |
| -------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `title`        | `text` (90, required), `subtitle` (120), `kicker` (32)                                                                                                           | The hook, a section turn, the call to action. Words rise in one by one; a bar draws under.                                                   |
| `list`         | `items` (1 to 6, text or `{text, on}`, 60 each), `heading`, `numbered`, `layout` (`spotlight` for 4 or fewer by default, `stack`)                                | A few named things. Spotlight shows one big item at a time with a progress rail.                                                             |
| `number`       | `value` (required), `label` (60, required), `from`, `prefix`, `unit`, `decimals`, `on`                                                                           | One number that matters. Counts up to exactly `value` and lands with a pulse.                                                                |
| `versus`       | `left` and `right` (`{title (24), points: 1 to 4 (40 each)}`), `title`, `verdict` (80)                                                                           | A choice between two things. Cards fly in from both sides, a VS badge, the verdict banner.                                                   |
| `steps`        | `steps` (2 to 6 of `{label (40), detail (80), on}`), `title`                                                                                                     | An ordered process. A timeline that fills as each step is said.                                                                              |
| `code`         | `language` (`js`, `ts`, `json`, `sh`, `py` and full names), `code` or `diff` (`{before, after}`), `title`, `file`, `focus` (`{lines: [a, b], on \| line \| at}`) | Real code. Lines type in, then the camera leans in on the focused lines. 16 lines and 48 characters at most; 12 and 40 read best on a phone. |
| `terminal`     | `lines` (1 to 8 of `{text (48), kind: cmd \| out, on}`), `prompt`, `title`                                                                                       | A command and its output. Commands type at 30 characters a second. Nothing is run.                                                           |
| `chart`        | `data` (2 to 10 of `{label (16), value, on}`), `type` (`bar`, `line`), `unit`, `title`                                                                           | Real numbers compared. The biggest bar is lit; values count up.                                                                              |
| `nodes`        | `nodes` (2 to 7 of `{id, label (20), on}`), `edges` (1 to 12 of `{from, to}`), `title`                                                                           | A small system or dependency graph, laid out in layers, with packets on each edge.                                                           |
| `request-flow` | made by `video-explain` `arch-import` (nodes with tiers, edges, steps)                                                                                           | A request travelling through a real architecture, step by step, with evidence.                                                               |
| `rate-limit`   | a simulation spec (`seed`, `count` up to 400, `pattern`, `algos`: 1 or 2), `title`                                                                               | The real algorithm run on the seed: requests fly to the gate, the bucket fills, 429s bounce.                                                 |
| `sequence`     | `source` (text: `A->B: text`, `B-->A: text`, `participant A as Name`, `Note over A,B: text`; up to 4 participants), `title`                                      | Who talks to whom, in order. Arrows draw as each row is said.                                                                                |
| `quote`        | `text` (240), `by`, `source`                                                                                                                                     | A real, attributed quote only.                                                                                                               |
| `broll`        | `asset` (an image or video id from `assets`), `fit`, `caption` (60)                                                                                              | The user's own image or clip, with a slow push-in.                                                                                           |

## Kinetic

| Kind      | Props                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           | What it does                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| --------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `kinetic` | `bg` (`black`, `dark`, `accent` = a slab), `phrases` (up to 12 groups of at most 4 words), `emph`, `exit` (`slide`, `drop`, `blur`, `shatter`, `none`), `y`, `cards` (up to 2 of `{title, rows: [{text, tag, bind}], x, y, tilt, at}`), `flow` (`{nodes: 2 to 5, at}`), `text` (only with no voice), `holdLast` | Each spoken word pops in on its start in Anton, key words in the accent colour with a glow and a punch of the camera. Without `emph`, the longest word of each phrase is lit. Made by `video-kinetic`'s planner; fine to use alone inside an explainer for a stakes or takeaway beat. |

## Showcase

| Kind     | Props                                                                                                                                                                 | What it does                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| -------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `screen` | `asset` (the recording), `device` (`browser`, `laptop`, `phone`, `none`), `viewport`, `zoom`, `cursor`, `ripples`, `title`, `callouts` (up to 8 of `{text (32), at}`) | The recording in an original device frame. On a vertical video a wide recording gets a taller window and the camera trails the cursor; zooms ease in and out; clicks ripple; callouts replace the title pill. Made by `video-showcase`'s `plan.mjs`. |

## Everywhere

- A living background (two drifting colour fields, a dot grid, a vignette), film grain and a slow push-in on every scene (a progress bar at the top is available with `style.progress`).
- Captions (explainer and showcase) sit at 70% of the height, above the platform's buttons; scenes keep their content between 11% and 64%.
- Sound effects are placed from the film: a whoosh on each transition, a soft pop when an item appears, an impact on the first scene and on slabs.
