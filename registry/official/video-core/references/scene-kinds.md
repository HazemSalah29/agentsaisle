# Scene kinds

Text is always shown as text, never as HTML. The first scene is fully visible at frame 0 (it moves in but does not fade), so the thumbnail is not empty.

| Kind           | Props                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     | Notes                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| -------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `title`        | `text` (required, 120 chars), `subtitle`                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  | Big line, optional second line that follows 0.25 s later.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| `quote`        | `text` (required, 300), `by`, `source`                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    | Accent bar on the reading-start side. Only quote real, attributed words.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| `list`         | `items` (1 to 8), `heading`, `numbered`                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   | With one narration line per item, each item appears with its line; otherwise they spread over the scene.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| `chart`        | `type` (`bar` or `line`), `data` (2 to 24 of `{label, value}`), `title`, `unit`                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           | Bars grow, a line draws itself. Values are plotted as given; negative values go below the baseline. Use real data only.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| `broll`        | `asset` (an id from `assets`), `fit` (`cover` or `contain`), `caption`                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    | Image or video (mp4, webm, mov). Stills get a slow push-in. A video's own sound is not used.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| `rate-limit`   | `seed`, `count` (1 to 600), `pattern` (`steady`, `poisson` or `burst` with `ratePerSec`; burst also `burstSize`, `burstEverySec`), `algos` (1 or 2 of `token-bucket` `capacity` `refillPerSec`; `leaky-bucket` `capacity` `leakPerSec`; `fixed-window` and `sliding-window` `limit` `windowSec`), `title`                                                                                                                                                 | Dots arrive, a meter fills and drains, rejected requests stop at the gate (429), a chart counts allowed against rejected. Drawn from the real algorithm run on the seed; results cannot be typed in, `check` refuses them. Two algorithms run on the same arrivals, side by side.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| `code`         | `language` (`javascript`, `typescript`, `json`, `bash`, `python`; short names `js`, `ts`, `sh`, `py` work), one of `code` or `diff` (`{before, after}`), `title`, `file`, `focus` (list of `{lines: [first, last], line}` or `{lines, at}`)                                                                                                                                                                                                               | Highlighted code (Shiki, github-dark, on its own dark panel). Lines appear one by one (up to 20 lines, 60 characters wide, 1,500 characters). `focus` dims the rest from a narration line (`line`, counting from 0) or a time (`at`, seconds). `diff` shows removed rows (red), then added rows (green) at the middle of the scene; line level, not token level. Uses the machine's monospace font (Menlo on macOS), so letter shapes can differ between machines.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| `sequence`     | `source` (text, 1,200 characters), `title`                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                | An animated sequence diagram. One line each: `A->B: text` (request, solid), `B-->A: text` (reply, dashed), `participant A as Browser`, `Note over A,B: text`. A subset of Mermaid also works (`sequenceDiagram`, `->>`, `-->>`). Up to 6 participants, 12 rows, 30 characters a label. Rows appear in order, each with its narration line when there is one narration line per row; earlier rows dim. `loop`, `alt`, `activate` and the like are refused with the line number.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| `request-flow` | `nodes` (2 to 10 of `{id, label, role, tier}`), `edges` (`{id, from, to}`), `steps` (1 to 14 of `{edge, reverse, label, together, evidence}` or `{event, node}`), `title`, `shown`                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        | Components laid out by tier, a packet travelling each connection in order, the step caption and its `file:line` evidence underneath. Do not write these by hand: `video-explain`'s `arch-import` makes them from an `arch-visualize` model (`docs/architecture.json`) and keeps only what the flow touches. The scene never reads a model file. An optional `ms` on a step is shown as given; never add one that was not measured.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| `screen`       | `asset` (the recording), `device` (`phone`, `laptop`, `browser` or `none`), `viewport` `{w, h}`, `zoom` (up to 40 of `{at, dur, x, y, scale}`), `cursor` (`{t, x, y}` points), `ripples` (`{t, x, y}`), `pip` (a webcam asset), `title`                                                                                                                                                                                                                   | A screen recording inside an original, generic device frame. The camera eases to each `zoom` target (x, y are 0 to 1 across the recording, scale 1 to 3, the view never leaves the recording); the cursor follows its points and each ripple marks a click. Times are in the scene's own clock, counted after speed-ups and cut waits were applied to the media. Do not write these by hand: `video-showcase`'s `plan.mjs` makes them from the capture's interaction log.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| `puppet`       | `cast` (1 to 4 of `{rig, x, y, height, flip, speaks, acts}`), `backdrop` `{preset}` (`meadow`, `room`, `night`)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           | Original SVG characters on a drifting flat backdrop. `rig` is a folder in `characters/` holding `puppet.json`. `x` is across the stage (0 to 1), `y` is the feet (0.2 to 1), `height` is a share of the scene height. `acts` are one at a time: `walk` (`to`), `wave`, `jump` (`lift`), `nod`, `dance`, `emote` (`mood`: neutral, happy, sad, surprised, sleepy), each with `at` and optional `dur`. `speaks` lists narration line ids whose mouth shapes come from `voice/visemes.json`. Blinks are automatic. Do not write rigs by hand: `video-kids` makes them. `look`: `"2d"` (default, a flat drawing from `puppet.json`) or `"3d"` (a lit, shadowed 3D character from `puppet3d.json`, drawn with three.js; the same acts, moods, speech and backdrop names). 3D needs WebGL: fast with a GPU, about 6 times slower without one. |
| `timeline`     | `title`, `events` (2 to 10 of `{at, label, type, where}`; `at` is a time as text up to 12 characters, `label` up to 60, `where` up to 24, `type` is `failure`, `alert`, `action`, `fix` or `info`)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        | A vertical timeline. Events appear one at a time, each with its narration line when there is one line per event; earlier events dim. The type is also written as a word (FAILED, ALERT, ACTION, FIXED, INFO). The times are text you were given; the scene never computes or invents them.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| `shot`         | `layers` (1 to 4 of `{asset, role: bg                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     | subject                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 | overlay, depth 0..1}`), `camera` (`{move: push | pull | pan-l | pan-r | tilt-up | tilt-down | whip-l | whip-r | orbit | hold, from, to, ease, ramp: [start, end, speed]}`), `type`(up to 3 of`{text, at, style: slam | stagger | glitch | tag | price, anchor, size}`), `cards`(up to 3 of`{text, sub, at, x, y, style: pill | card}`), `fx` (`grain`, `vignette`, `grade: punch | warm | cool | mono | film`, and up to 8 times each for `glitch`, `flash`, `shake`), `enter` (`{style: cut | flash | whip-l | whip-r | zoom | glitch, dur}`) | One beat of a product or launch edit from the user's own photos: layers move at different speeds for a 2.5D parallax, the camera moves and can speed-ramp, type and cards pop in, and grain, flashes, shakes and RGB-ghost glitches land on the times you give. Full-bleed. Flashes must be at least 0.4 s apart. No script or WebGL in the page. |
| `kinetic`      | `bg` (`black`, `dark`, `accent`), `grid`, `vignette`, `grain`, `phrases` (up to 12 groups of at most 4 words, matched to the spoken words in order; default: split at punctuation), `emph` (key words), `exit` (`slide`, `drop`, `blur`, `shatter`, `none`), `holdLast`, `y`, `caption`, `cards` (up to 2 of `{title, rows: [{text, tag, bind}], x, y, tilt, at}`), `flow` (`{nodes: 2 to 5 labels, at}`), `text` (used only when the scene has no voice) | Speech-synced bold type. Each word of the scene's narration pops in at the moment it is spoken, in the Anton display font (SIL OFL); key words take the accent colour with a glow and a spark ring; a phrase leaves just before the next arrives (the last one stays so a transition can split it). Optional running caption top left, 3D-tilted cards whose rows appear on spoken words, and a node flow with a travelling dot. Needs `voice/words.json` for real timing; without voice it spreads `text` evenly.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| `calligraphy`  | `text` (up to 80 characters, 10 words), `translation`, `by`, `font` (a family from `brand/fonts`, by file name), `reveal` (`wipe`, `fade` or `rise`)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      | Lettering revealed word by word in the text's reading direction (a wipe starts on the right for Arabic). Words are real text, so the browser shapes and joins the letters; a word is never split. Control and direction-mark characters and pre-shaped (presentation form) letters are refused. The font is not shipped: put an OFL font such as Amiri, Aref Ruqaa or Reem Kufi in `brand/fonts`, or the brand font is used.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |

`custom` is accepted by the schema but not built yet; render refuses it.
