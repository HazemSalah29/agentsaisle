# Hooks

The hook is the first line of `script.md` (`beat: hook`) and should land its payoff within about 3 seconds of speech (roughly 7 words; the check warns above 4 s).

Good openers:

- The payoff first: "A bucket with no tokens is why your API says 429."
- A surprising fact you can source (put it in `facts.md`).
- A counter-take: "Most people think X. Actually Y."
- A visual question the first scene answers.

Avoid generic-curiosity hooks ("You won't believe...") and "storytime" intros; the check flags them. One 2026 analysis reports their completion rate fell about 40% year on year (`TODO(verify)`).

`hooks.md` holds 5 alternatives, numbered, each a full first line. The script uses one; the rest are for the user to swap.

Retention: something new on screen or in the idea every 1.5 to 3 seconds in shorts; open a loop early and close it before the call to action; the call to action comes after the payoff.
