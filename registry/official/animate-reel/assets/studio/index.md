# Index

The map of this folder. Claude adds a line here whenever it files something new.

## Context

- [[brand]]: your page, voice and colours.

- [[taste]]: what worked and what did not.

## Notes

- `00 - notes/ideas/`: one note per idea. See `00 - notes/ideas/_index`.

- `00 - notes/content/`: one folder per reel.

- `00 - notes/content/sample-souls-like-soldiers/`: a finished sample reel to open and play (it comes in the separate Samples download: put the folder here). It shows what a reel folder looks like when every step is done.
- `00 - notes/content/sample-souls-like-soldiers-carousel/`: a finished sample carousel, made from that reel (also in the Samples download). Open the PNGs and `caption.txt` to see what a carousel folder looks like when every step is done.

- `00 - notes/sources/`: raw sources, never edited.

- [[ledger]]: one row per post, with numbers.

## Output

- `03 - export/`: finished videos.
